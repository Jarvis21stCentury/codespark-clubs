Why this exists

We were the students trying to start a club with nothing to hand out.

Starting a club is mostly paperwork and dead time before anyone writes a line of code. Room bookings. A sponsor teacher. Something to actually do at the first meeting when eleven people show up.

CodeSpark hands you that part finished. The curriculum, the posters, the first meetings planned out, and a network of 128+ schools across eight countries running the same material.

About us

Built by students

CodeSpark started in 2025 when a few students hit the same wall trying to get tech clubs off the ground at their own schools.

It began as help for one AI club. It's now an online platform with starter kits, guides and coding projects running in eight countries.

  1. Start

    Helping one AI club get its first meetings on the calendar.

  2. Next

    An online platform with starter kits, guides and coding projects.

  3. Then

    Multiple schools running the same curriculum.

  4. Then

    Partnership tools so clubs could work together.

  5. Now

    Eight countries, 128+ schools, 2,700+ students.
